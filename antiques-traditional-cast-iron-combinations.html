Cast iron combinations

Combinations include a built-in shelf and work as a standalone piece without an additional mantel. These are often installed in smaller spaces like bedrooms, where a full mantel setup would take up too much space. Combinations are suitable for use as an open fire or can be fitted with a made to measure gas fire.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers, as well as one-off pieces in our showroom. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.
