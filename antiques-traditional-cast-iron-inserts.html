Cast iron inserts

Cast iron inserts are the focal point of most traditional fireplaces. They need to be paired with a mantel and are suitable for use as an open fire or can be fitted with a made to measure gas fire.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers as well as one-off pieces in our showroom. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.
