Antique & traditional fireplaces

We offer a range of traditional fireplaces, with both antique and reproduction pieces available. We have a wide variety of pieces from inserts and mantels to registers and baskets. These are suitable for use as an open fire and most can also be fitted with a made to measure gas fire.

Cast iron combinations

Combinations include a built-in shelf and work as a standalone piece without an additional mantel. These are often installed in smaller spaces like bedrooms, where a full mantel setup would take up too much space. Combinations are suitable for use as an open fire or can be fitted with a made to measure gas fire.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers, as well as one-off pieces in our showroom. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.

Cast iron inserts

Cast iron inserts are the focal point of most traditional fireplaces. They need to be paired with a mantel and are suitable for use as an open fire or can be fitted with a made to measure gas fire.
