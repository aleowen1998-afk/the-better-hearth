Electric stoves

Electric stoves are freestanding and can be placed inside of a mantel as part of a traditional setup or installed as standalone units. Our electric stoves are made from the same high-quality cast iron construction as our wood stoves.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.
