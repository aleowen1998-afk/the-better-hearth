Traditional inset gas fires

These high-efficiency inset gas fires are made to fit with traditional-style setups, bringing modern technology to traditional design. Many of these can be installed into an existing setup and we also offer them as part of a larger installation where we supply the whole fireplace.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.

Capital provident he, inset gas fire

The Provident is a contemporary decorative gas fire with a coal effect fuel bed. Available with slide control enabling you to light and control the fire without having to remove the ashpan cover.

By integrating the matching Provident Back Panel (available in Black or Highlight), we are able to house higher efficiency gas fires. Closing off the unit and adding a canopy to the Fireframe means we are able to increase the efficiency of the fires and ensure that the heat output is going into the room and not up the chimney.
