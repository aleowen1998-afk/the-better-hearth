Mantels

We offer many mantels in a wide range of styles and sizes. Our most popular options are made from white marble, but we offer many other materials such as limestone, wood and cast iron. In addition to our standard materials, we often have one-off pieces and we have some more unusual materials available on request. Please contact us for more information or visit our showroom where we have lots of mantels on display.

Most mantels come in a range of standard sizes, but we offer a made to measure service in certain materials.

Marble mantels

Marble is a traditional material that has been used to make fireplaces for hundreds of years. We have a wide range of white marble and Carrara marble mantels, as well as more unusual colours available on request.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers as well as one-off pieces in our showroom. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.

Wooden mantels

Wooden mantels are a traditional and more affordable alternative to stone mantels. They also have the flexibility of being made to size as standard and they can be painted.

In addition to the options below, we have more available from a variety of suppliers as well as one-off pieces in our showroom. Please contact us or see our suppliers page for more information.

Stove beam, rustic Geocast

Our non-combustible oak effect beams have been developed from a natural and extremely durable material and are moulded to achieve ultimate high definition creating a truly authentic look. Being non-combustible, our Geocast beams allow smaller clearance distances than natural wood.

Beautifully rustic yet sophisticated with a natural and dark oak finish, the Geocast Rustic Beam embraces a rural, country charm look and feel, perfect for the traditional home.
