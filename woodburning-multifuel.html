Woodburning and multifuel stoves

Woodburning stoves are enclosed and ideal for heating even large rooms. Usually made from cast iron, they burn with a high efficiency, bringing the comfort and warmth of a real flame to your home. We offer a wide range of stoves, all of which conform to the latest DEFRA regulations and are approved for installation in smoke control areas like London.

Many of our stoves are also available as multifuel models, capable of burning both wood and smokeless coal.
